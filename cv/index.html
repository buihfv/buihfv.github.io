    First-principles modeling of materials for energy and electronic devices, advised by Prof. Heonjae Jeong.
    • Thermodynamic stability and Li-ion conductivity of doped LLZO solid-electrolyte interfaces (Jul 2026 – present): evaluating Al-, Ga-, Ta-, and Sr+Ta-doped LLZO at LiSi anode and FeF₃ cathode interfaces for thermal-battery applications; assessing dopant-dependent interfacial phase stability and Li-ion transport at realistic operating conditions (500 °C) from first principles.
