25.12 – present

Undergraduate Researcher

Materials Design and Process Engineering Laboratory, Sogang University · Seoul, Republic of Korea

First-principles modeling of materials for energy and electronic devices, advised by Prof. Heonjae Jeong.

  • Thermodynamic stability and Li-ion conductivity of doped LLZO solid-electrolyte interfaces (Jul 2026 – present): evaluating Al-, Ga-, Ta-, and Sr+Ta-doped LLZO at LiSi anode and FeF₃ cathode interfaces for thermal-battery applications; assessing dopant-dependent interfacial phase stability and Li-ion transport at realistic operating conditions (500 °C) from first principles.
  • Built a ~350-atom FeF₃/LLZO coherent interface model at ~8.5% lattice mismatch; diagnosed and resolved relaxation failures traced to sheared-supercell geometry, short F–Fe contacts, and vacuum-layer artifacts.
  • Origin of negative photoconductivity in amorphous ZnON (Feb – Aug 2026): performed first-principles calculations to explain negative photoconductivity in amorphous ZnON; identified charge-localization mechanisms at the valence band maximum and conduction band minimum using AIMD and hybrid DFT.
  • Evaluating universal machine-learning interatomic potentials (MACE) as a validated substitute for AIMD on ~600-atom systems across ten dopant variants.
  • Maintained VASP workflows on a KISTI supercomputer and a shared GPU server, including job submission, monitoring, and backup automation.
