Inverse design searches composition space and proposes materials that should carry a target property. A proposal is not yet a material: it becomes one only once it has been synthesized and measured.

This project is the group's first experimental workflow, and its purpose is to make the loop close: calculate, synthesize, measure, and feed the measurement back. β-PbSnF₄ is the benchmark rather than the goal. It is the best-characterised fluoride-ion conductor available, so its ionic conductivity is a known number — which makes it the right material to test a workflow against. If the sample we make reproduces the reference value, the route and the measurement are trustworthy enough to use on a composition nobody has made before.
