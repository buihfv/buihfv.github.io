A camera slider built from an aluminum block and a linear rail, driven by a linear-actuator motor under Arduino control for repeatable tracking shots.

**Duration** March 2025 – June 2025 · **Course** Machine Production Practice · **Role** TODO_YOUR_ROLE · **Team** TODO_TEAM_SIZE

## Motivation

A tracking shot needs motion that is slow, even and repeatable — exactly what a hand cannot do and a motor can. The constraint of the course was that the frame had to be machined rather than bought, so the design had to be manufacturable with the tools on hand.

## Approach

- **Frame.** Designed the linear-rail slider in 3D CAD, machined from an aluminum block with bolted joints.
- **Drive.** Selected and mounted a linear-actuator motor sized to carry the camera along the rail.
- **Control.** Arduino-based motor control regulating speed, direction and reciprocating motion, so a pass can be repeated identically.
